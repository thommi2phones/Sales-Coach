What the Data Shows

✅ Two straight revenue months at 86–87%

The April "worst month in some time" is firmly behind. $40,605 closed, 87% of quota, right on the heels of May's 86%. Back-to-back near quota is durable performance and the right headline to lead with.

✅ Pipeline-gen recovered to ~96%

After May's ~54% scare, ~$179k created / ~$7.8k avg ACV is a real rebound. The inbound funnel and expansion motion (BRP, Tracer, YMCA, Promega, CFG) are both healthy and quietly carrying the month.

⚠️ Self-generated pipeline is near zero

Contact creation halved (90 vs 177) and calls hit zero. The ~$179k created is inbound + expansion — there is currently no outbound floor under the number if inbound demand softens. That's a Q3 revenue risk hiding behind a green number.

🔴 Calls went to zero — a two-month failure now

39 → 0, against ~22 Call Prep blocks that Reclaim held nearly every business day. This was the explicit #1 MIT of the June plan and it moved the wrong direction. The block is not the fix — this needs a hard, measured daily dial target with weekly accountability.

🔴 Every $10k+ deal closed lost

Unilever ($40k), AdRoll ($13k), AMN ($12k), Celebrands ($12k) all closed lost. The MEDDICC breadth the May plan called for didn't reach the big pipe. Revenue survived on <$5k breadth — that doesn't scale to quota in a lean-inbound month.

🔴 Stale deals still dying at 300+ days

Unilever (created 8/2025) and AdRoll (created 6/2025) are June's Industry-Dive repeat — ~10–12 month cycles that never had a real next step. The day-60 disqualification rule still isn't being enforced; zombies inflate pipe, then die loud.
