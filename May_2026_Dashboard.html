What the Data Shows

✅ The MEDDICC motion converts

Scotiabank ($35k) closed after a 421-day cycle: multi-threaded into engineering, ran a technical SE call, navigated security review, engaged the EB. This is the disciplined enterprise motion working end-to-end. Replicate the playbook on US Soccer and BT1D.

✅ Revenue recovered hard (37% → 86%)

May fixed April's revenue problem — $40.4k closed, 86% of quota. AI-led velocity (Flor/Claude, fast MCP close) plus one disciplined enterprise win is a repeatable two-engine model.

⚠️ Pipeline-gen arc broke (103% → 54%)

After four months building to a 103% peak, May fell to ~54% of the $187k goal. Avg ACV dropped to $5.05k from $8.4k. Breadth held (20 deals, +16% new contacts) but deal size and total credit shrank.

⚠️ The Call Prep block isn't producing dials

18 Call Prep blocks were scheduled and held — but only 39 dials happened (vs 280 in April). The calendar structure exists; the calls aren't being made inside it. This is the #1 fixable lever for June.

🔴 Net closed activity −$85k

$40k won vs $125k lost across 18 deals. DTCC ($25k, April flagship) and Industry Dive ($20k, 262-day stale death) lead the losses. Both Upsells (Chefman, Maxio = $24k) died the same month they were created.

🔴 Two loss patterns to autopsy

Stale death (Industry Dive 262d, Havas 138d, Organicare 99d) = no exit discipline. Instant flameout (Diablo 14d, Maxio 13d, Chefman/Parc Safari 0d) = premature creation or poor qualification. Different root causes, different fixes.
