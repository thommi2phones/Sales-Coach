## Where to Improve

### 1. 🔴 Deal Amounts Missing — Forecasting Is Blind
**The problem:** At least 12 active deals have no amount set in HubSpot. Deals like Vooks, Havas Edge, US Soccer, Havas DS, Upwork, DTCC, OMI, Organicare, Industry Dive have $0 or null. When Andrew looks at your pipeline, he can't see the real value.

**Why it matters:** Pipeline calls and forecasting are based on amounts. If your $20K Industry Dive deal shows as $0, your pipeline looks weaker than it is. You're selling yourself short.

**Action:** Audit and populate amounts on all open deals before your next 1:1. Even rough estimates.

### 2. 🔴 Stale Close Dates = Pipeline Trust Erosion
**The problem:**
- Paxos | Claude | $1.7k — close date was April 9. It's now April 29. Still in "Agreement." 20 days overdue with no close.
- Industry Dive | MIC+BQ | $20k — created August 2025. Close date June 2026. Has been in pipeline 8+ months.
- US Soccer — created January 2026. Still at 20% probability, May close.
- Several expired deals from 2023/2024 showing in the pipeline view.

**Why it matters:** Stale close dates destroy pipeline credibility. Andrew can't trust the data to make decisions. Every week a deal stays past close date without an update is a signal that next steps aren't locked.

**Action:** Before the 1:1, for every deal with a past close date: either close it out, or update the close date with a documented reason.

### 3. 🟡 $36k Gap to Goal — Conversion Is the Lever
81% is good but not 100%. The gap isn't an activity problem (the effort is there). It's a conversion problem — deals are getting created but not all are closing on schedule.

**Why it matters:** The deals to close the gap already exist in pipeline. Scotiabank ($40K), US Soccer ($40K), DTCC ($25K), AMN ($12K) are all sitting mid-funnel. Advancing one of those closes the $36k gap entirely.

**Action:** Identify which mid-funnel deal is most likely to close in May and build a specific mutual close plan for it. Bring this to the 1:1.

### 5. 🟡 Large Deals Stalling in Early Stages
Tracer | MGMT API | $100K has been sitting at Qualification since it was created. US Soccer at $40K has been in Qualified since January. These are the deals that will make or break Q2 — but at 20% probability with August/May close dates, they're not progressing.

**Action:** Identify the specific blocker for Tracer and US Soccer. Is there an economic buyer involved? What's the mutual action plan? Bring a status update to the 1:1.

### 6. 🟡 Email-to-Call Ratio Is Skewed
979 emails vs 280 calls (3.5:1 ratio). Email is efficient for prospecting at volume, but live conversations create urgency and reveal objections that emails can't surface.

**Action:** For all mid-funnel deals (Qualified stage and above), prioritize a live conversation over another email. If a deal has been in Qualified for 30+ days and the last touch was an email — it's not progressing.
