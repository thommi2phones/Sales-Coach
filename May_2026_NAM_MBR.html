Slide 4b · The Win

Scotiabank — Anatomy of the MEDDICC Win

$35k closed · 421-day cycle · Cross-sell · the deal the April plan was built around. This is the template for every enterprise deal going forward.

What we did right

  • Multi-threaded late-stage into their engineering org — Director of Engineering + Lead & Senior Solutions Architects
  • Ran a technical SE call with a written prep brief (5/27) — anticipated SFTP vs API vs Hub/BigQuery objections
  • Got security/DR docs into their vendor review early (4/24–4/28, confirmed received) — not a last-minute blocker
  • Kept the EB + decision path live through signature

Why it converted

  • Their engineering team tried to revalidate the ingestion method right before signature — and we were ready
  • The security story ("we drop files inside your perimeter") fit a regulated bank's posture
  • Google partner motion (Noel → Scott @ Google) gave an extra lever on the BigQuery option
  • The April lesson from Uniphore (no EB → lost) was inverted here (EB engaged → won)

The replicable playbook

  • Late-stage = multi-thread into technical/engineering, not just the champion
  • Every technical call gets a prep brief
  • Push security/legal docs early, before they're asked for
  • So what: this is now the standard for Upwork, Grafton, AMBSE, BlueDoor, BT1D — June's $10k+ pipe
