## 3 Wins

1. **Two straight months near quota — 86% → 87%.** The April slump is firmly behind; back-to-back is durable performance.
2. **Pipeline gen recovered to ~96%** ($179k / 23 deals / ~$7.8k avg) after May's ~54% scare — inbound + expansion both healthy.

## 3 Gaps

1. **Calls went to zero.** 22 Call Prep blocks → 0 dials. This was the explicit #1 MIT of the June plan and it moved backward. **The block isn't the fix — I need a measured daily dial target.**
2. **Every $10k+ deal closed lost.** Unilever $40k, AdRoll $13k, AMN $12k, Celebrands $12k. Revenue survived on <$5k breadth — the $10k+ MEDDICC motion (May MIT #2) didn't reach them.

2. **Convert the $10k+ pipe — stop living on <$5k breadth.** June hit 87% on 11 small deals + one carryover while every $10k+ deal died. Run the full Scotiabank-style MEDDICC/EB/security motion across Upwork, RLE, Shubert, TaxAct, BlueDoor. Enforce day-60 disqualification (the Unilever/AdRoll lesson).

## MEDDICC — The Conversion Lever (Proven on Scotiabank, Missing in June)

**What Scotiabank taught us (and June forgot):**
- Multi-thread into the technical/economic org, not just the champion
- Run a prep brief before every technical call
- Get security/DR docs into review **early**
- Keep the EB + decision path live through close

**June's $10k+ losses had none of this.** Unilever and AdRoll died stale; AMN never got an EB; Celebrands entered and died unqualified.
