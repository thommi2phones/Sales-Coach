⚠ The Single Most Coachable Item of the Month

~18 Daily Call Prep blocks on the calendar → 39 dials made. That is roughly 2 dials per block. The block was scheduled, defended, and honored — Thomas showed up to the time. This is not a calendar problem (the calendar was excellent) and not a discipline problem at the block level — it's the gap between "block scheduled" and "dial made." Closing that gap is the entire top-of-funnel recovery plan for June.

🎯 May Execution Lessons → June Plan

1
Close the block-to-dial gap — the #1 coachable item

~18 Call Prep blocks produced only 39 dials. The calendar is already perfect; the work inside it isn't happening. Set a minimum-dials-per-block target (e.g. 8) and track dials made inside each Reclaim block against the block itself. This single metric is the entire top-of-funnel recovery.

Daily · starting June 1
2
Bank the Scotiabank MEDDICC playbook as the template

Multi-thread into engineering, written SE prep brief that pre-answers technical objections, security/DR docs early, EB held to signature. Write this up as the named "$10k+ deal motion" and apply it to every open $10k+ deal (Liberty, Charter One, BT1D). It just delivered $35k — make it repeatable.

Due June 8
3
Don't let a closing month empty the funnel

The Scotiabank close was worth it, but calls went 280 → 39 and pipe gen fell to ~54%. Protect a non-negotiable daily dial floor even during heads-down close weeks, so June doesn't inherit an empty top of funnel the way May inherited April's. Revenue and pipe cannot trade off month to month.

Apply this week

Run MEDDICC on DTCC and Industry Dive losses — the $45k that got away without it

DTCC ($25k, 28d) and Industry Dive ($20k, 262d) were lost with no EB and no technical motion — the exact opposite of Scotiabank. One-page autopsy each: where was the EB, where was the SE call, and why did MEDDICC apply to one $25k deal but not these two?
