## The Headline: 87% on Breadth, Zero on the Phones

**Revenue: $40,605 / $46,750 = 87%** — the best two-month revenue stretch in some time. But the *composition* is the coaching story:

- **No anchor deal.** May had Scotiabank ($35k) carrying the month. June had no single deal over $10k. The biggest win, **AMBSE ($9,999)**, was the April "main swing" and May carryover — it finally closed, but it was already on the board. The other 11 wins were all ≤$4.9k (Growth/MCP/Starter tier).
- **The $10k+ pipe died instead of closing.** Every deal ≥$10k that reached a close in June closed **lost**: Unilever Prestige ($40k), AdRoll ($13k), AMN ($12k), Celebrands ($12k). The May MIT — "run the full Scotiabank-style MEDDICC motion across every $10k+ deal" — did not happen at breadth.
- **Delivered on 0 logged dials.** The month's revenue was carried by inbound and expansion. Calls logged fell from 39 (May) to **0** (June) — against ~22 Reclaim "Daily Call Prep" blocks on the calendar. The structure held; the dials didn't happen inside it, again, more completely than in May.

**So what:** June is not the recovery it looks like on the scorecard. It's a **second month of living off the base** — expansion revenue and a full inbound funnel — while the outbound engine stays stone cold and the large deals leak out the bottom. That's a Q3 revenue risk hiding behind a green revenue number.
