June held the revenue line for a second straight month — $40,605 / 87% — and pipe generation recovered hard to ~96% (~$179k across 23 deals). On the scorecard it reads like the "both engines on" month the May plan called for. The reality underneath is the story of this MBR: the number was carried by breadth, not big deals (11 of 12 wins ≤$4.9k, and the largest — AMBSE at $10k — was the April/May carryover finally closing), while every $10k+ deal that closed, closed lost. And it was all delivered on zero logged dials against ~22 Reclaim Call Prep blocks. May's block-to-dial gap (18 blocks → 39 dials) didn't get fixed — it went total (22 blocks → 0). June didn't recover the outbound engine; it papered over it with inbound volume and expansion.

⚠ The Single Most Coachable Item of the Month — Now a Two-Month Trend

~22 Daily Call Prep blocks on the calendar → 0 dials logged. This is no longer an anomaly — it's a two-month trend that got worse. May's block-to-dial gap was partial (18 blocks → 39 dials, ~2 per block); June's is total (22 blocks → 0). The block was scheduled, defended, and honored every business day — but the work it was reserved for did not happen inside it, at all. This was the explicit #1 MIT of the June plan and it moved the wrong direction. The lesson is now unambiguous: the block is not the fix. The July plan pairs every Call Prep block with a hard, measured ≥15-dial/day target and weekly 1:1 accountability — because a placeholder on the calendar does nothing on its own.
