## Where to Improve

### 1. 🔴 Calls Collapsed — 280 → 39
18 Call Prep blocks produced 39 dials. The block is on the calendar; the dials aren't happening inside it. **This is the #1 driver of the pipeline-gen drop and the single most coachable metric.**

### 2. 🔴 Pipeline Generation Fell to ~54%
20 small deals (~$5k avg), two of the biggest lost same-month. After April's 103%, the YTD pipe-gen arc (50 → 90 → 89 → 103 → **~54**) broke. This is a **July/August revenue risk**, not a May problem.

### 3. 🔴 MEDDICC Was Not Applied Broadly
Scotiabank won with the full motion; DTCC ($25k) and Industry Dive ($20k) lost without it. Industry Dive sat 262 days — the 60-day disqualification rule would have killed it months ago. **The discipline that won the flagship needs to cover the whole $10k+ pipe.**

### 4. 🟡 Concentration + Overdue Close Dates
US Soccer ($40k) is already overdue (May 28). June's number leans on a handful of $15k+ deals (Upwork, Grafton, AMBSE, BT1D). If two slip, June misses.
