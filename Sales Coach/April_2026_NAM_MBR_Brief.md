## MEDDICC — The Conversion Lever

**Documents produced per deal:**
- MEDDICC Scorecard (HubSpot field, R/Y/G on 8 dims, refresh every 14d)
- Value Narrative (1-page, every $10k+ deal before Proposal)
- Mutual Action Plan (shared w/ buyer, dates+owners through close)
- Executive Summary ($20k+ deals, vehicle for the EB conversation)

**What changes in conversations:**
- Discovery → quantified Metric + named Pain owner
- 2nd call → Champion test ("would you bring this to your VP?") + Decision Criteria
- Every $10k+ deal → EB conversation booked **before** sending pricing (the Uniphore lesson)
