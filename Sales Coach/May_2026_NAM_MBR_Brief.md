## MEDDICC — The Conversion Lever (Proven on Scotiabank)

**What Scotiabank taught us (replicate it):**
- Multi-thread into the technical/engineering org late-stage, not just the champion
- Run a **prep brief** before every technical call (we did; it worked)
- Get security/DR docs into their review **early** so it's not a last-minute blocker
- Keep the EB + economic decision path live through close
