🎯 April Execution Lessons → May Plan

1
Run the Uniphore loss autopsy — the headline miss

$40k lost in 69 days. Was discovery shallow? Did you have an Economic Buyer? When did the deal stop progressing? This is the largest single April loss — one page, bring to the 1:1.

Due May 5
2
No more April-1-style cleanup days in Q2

$85k of April losses (Downstream, Equitable Life, Poco Bero, Tellus, IRIS) all died on Apr 1 — Q1 carryover that should have been dispositioned in March. Set a Friday-4pm hygiene block: any deal with no live activity in 14 days gets a real disposition call now, not at month-end.

Weekly · starting May 2
3
Codify the Paxos/DQ/Cousin Labs win pattern

April's wins were existing customers, simple expansions, or fast MCP/AI plays. Write a 1-page qualification checklist: "Day 1 of a winnable Thomas deal looks like ___." Use it on every new April-2 deal that came in (DTCC, StayTuned, Revibe, etc.).

Due May 9
4
4 fast losses = 4 missed qualification questions

Speed Sport (11d), Oversight (11d), Fusable (24d), Chefman (26d) all died inside 30 days. For each, write what discovery question would have surfaced the no-fit on call 1. Add those questions to your discovery deck.

Due May 12
5
Apply MEDDPICC to DTCC, StayTuned, Revibe — the three to protect

April's pipeline gave you three $20k+ deals (DTCC, StayTuned, Revibe = $65k credit). April's execution data shows large deals are exactly where you lose. Score all three across MEDDPICC, identify reds, and build an Economic Buyer plan for each before they hit 60 days.

Due May 12 · Bring to 1:1

60-day rule on $10k+ open deals

If a $10k+ deal is past 60 days without a confirmed Economic Buyer, paper process started, or business case documented — disposition it. Comcast (137d), MNP (425d), Downstream (390d) = the cost of not having this rule.
