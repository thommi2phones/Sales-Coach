Coaching Analysis

1. You're Losing Big, Winning Small

Your 10 new-biz wins averaged $5,844 each (Asana was a split deal at $4,444). Meanwhile, your 15 losses included at least 5 deals in the $12k-$30k range — Equitable Life ($30k), Downstream Casino ($30k), Z Supply ($17k), Poco Bero ($15k), and Quest ($15k). The estimated lost value (~$146k) is more than 2.5x what you closed.

This is the single biggest lever for you. Converting even 2-3 of those larger deals would have doubled your month. We need to dig into why those specific deals were lost — was it pricing, product fit, competition, or losing the internal champion? I'd recommend doing a deal autopsy on Equitable Life and Downstream Casino specifically.

2. End-of-Quarter Dump Is Expensive

6 of your 15 losses were closed-lost on March 31 — the last day of Q1. This suggests deals were being kept open for as long as possible hoping they'd close, then batch-cleaned at quarter end. Equitable Life ($30k), Poco Bero ($15k), Tellus ($12k), Downstream Casino ($30k), and IRIS ($1.2k) all died on the final day.

Coaching takeaway: earlier disqualification = more time on winnable deals. If a deal isn't progressing by mid-month, it's better to have an honest "are we still on track?" conversation than carry dead weight into the last week.

3. Activity Volume Is Strong — Efficiency Is the Question

813 emails, 770 tasks, 114 calls, 61 meetings — you are not coasting. That's serious hustle. But your call connect rate is only ~21% (24 connected out of 114 dials), and average call duration was just 13 seconds. Most calls were quick dials that didn't land.

Consider: Are you calling at the right times? Are you using a multi-channel sequence (call → email → LinkedIn) to warm prospects before dialing? Your LinkedIn activity (89 messages + 54 connection requests) suggests you're already multi-threading — make sure the timing is coordinated.

4. Cycle Times Are All Over the Map

Your wins ranged from 5 days (1800 Lighting, an XDR handoff) to 363 days (Asana). The sweet spot was 10-39 days for most wins. Meanwhile some losses lingered 40-112 days before dying. The Downstream Casino deal was open for over a year before closing lost.

For your 1-3 month cycle, deals that pass 60 days without advancing to a late stage should get a hard qualification review. Build a "deal health check" at the 30-day and 60-day marks.

5. Your Win Rate by Deal Size Tells a Story

Rough breakdown from the data:

Under $5k: Won 5, Lost 6 → ~45% win rate
$5k-$10k: Won 2, Lost 0 → 100% win rate
$10k+: Won 2, Lost 9 → ~18% win rate

You're crushing mid-market deals but struggling on larger ones. The $10k+ deals likely involve more stakeholders, longer procurement, and more competition. This is where multi-threading, getting executive sponsors, and starting paper processes early becomes critical.
