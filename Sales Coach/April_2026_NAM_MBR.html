Slide 13b · MEDDICC

MEDDICC — How May Deals Get Driven

April had pipe but couldn't close. The conversion lever is MEDDICC discipline — operationalize it, don't just name-check it. This is the bridge between "pipeline at 103%" and "revenue at 37%": deal management was the missing piece, MEDDICC is how we fix it.

The 8 Dimensions — in a Supermetrics deal

  • Metrics — quantified value: "saves 10 hrs/wk", "$X attributable revenue from attribution"
  • Economic Buyer — confirmed name + budget authority + direct conversation booked
  • Decision Criteria — written list of what they need to see to say yes
  • Decision Process — steps from now → contract, with owners + dates
  • Paper Process — procurement, legal, security, finance routes mapped
  • Identify Pain — quantified, urgent, owned, top-3 priority
  • Champion — willing to sell internally, has capital, tested
  • Competition — incumbent or alternatives named; "do nothing" included

Documents we produce per deal

  • MEDDICC Scorecard — HubSpot deal field or Google Doc · R/Y/G on each of 8 dims · updated every 14 days
  • Value Narrative (1-page) — pain → impact → metrics → recommendation · Coaching Goal 1
  • Mutual Action Plan (MAP) — shared with buyer · dates + owners through close · re-introduce after March's MSP failure
  • Executive Summary — for $20k+ deals · the vehicle for the EB conversation · Coaching Goal 3

What changes in conversations

  • Discovery call: must surface 1 quantified Metric + named Pain owner
  • 2nd call: Champion test ("would you bring this to your VP?") + Decision Criteria asked explicitly
  • Proposal stage: MAP shared as a working doc, not after the fact
  • Every $10k+ deal: direct EB conversation booked before sending pricing
    • April lost Uniphore $40k partly because we never met the EB
