MEDDICC — Replicate the Scotiabank Motion

Scotiabank proved MEDDICC converts flagship deals — and June forgot it. Every $10k+ deal that closed in June closed lost because the motion never reached them. The July job is breadth: run the exact Scotiabank motion on every $10k+ deal, or the pattern hits a fourth month.

What won Scotiabank

  • Economic Buyer — engaged + decision path live through signature
  • Technical multi-thread — engineering org, not just champion
  • Paper Process — security/DR docs delivered early, confirmed received
  • Prep brief before the technical call — objections anticipated

What lost June's $10k+ deals

  • Unilever + AdRoll died stale — no next step, 300+ days
  • AMN never got an EB (April pipeline, months live)
  • Celebrands entered and died unqualified in ~10 days
  • Pure-email follow-up · 60-day rule not enforced

July operating rhythm

  • Day 1: open MEDDICC card, rate R/Y/G
  • Every $10k+ deal: EB booked before pricing
  • Every technical call: written prep brief
  • Friday: disqualify anything 60d+ with no next step
